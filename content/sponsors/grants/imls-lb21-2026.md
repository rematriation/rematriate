bio: >-
  The Institute of Museum and Library Services is the primary source of federal support for the nation's libraries and museums. They advance, support, and empower America's museums, libraries, and related organizations through grantmaking, research, and policy development. IMLS envisions a nation where individuals and communities have access to museums and libraries to learn from and be inspired by the trusted information, ideas, and stories they contain about our diverse natural and cultural heritage.
purpose: >-
  We used this funding to design and test a two-week workshop that centers capacity-building for integral community members invested in archival interventions with their local goals to preserve, organize, and share their knowledge and data. During the training, community members learned how to organize and inventory archival materials, digitize photos and recordings, create descriptions so others can find and understand those materials, and upload items into an online community archive. To make this learning experience memorable, meaningful, materially supportive, and fun, we've designed this training as a “cold case” mystery investigation, recruited integral educational and resourceful community members from each village, paying them for their time and creativity, and partnered with the University of Alaska-Fairbanks to provide Continuing Education Credits for eligible participants.

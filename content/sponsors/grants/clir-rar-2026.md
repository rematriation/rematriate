bio: >-
  The Council on Library and Information Resources is an independent, nonprofit organization that forges strategies to enhance research, teaching, and learning environments in collaboration with libraries, cultural institutions, and communities of higher learning.
purpose: >-
  Aqqaluk Trust (AT) will digitize, steward, and make available an estimated 520 hours of audiovisual recordings of Inupiat between the 1970s through the 2010s. These audiovisual recordings document knowledge bearers speaking in their endangered language of Iñupiaq, traditional practices, and knowledges about their homeland and changes of it over time. This grant will digitize and preserve a collection currently held by AT by working with their longstanding multi-institutional team, The Rematriation Project (REMA), to inventory and create a Wikibase instance of the initial collection metadata for public access, and Bay Area Video Coalition Media for digitization. These deliverables will seed further impacts in preserving collections at risk, including a sharable educational model to develop and increase local capacities for sustainable access and preservation of digitized audiovisual materials.

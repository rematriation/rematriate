The team has created a prototype collection with a installation. This prototype provides us with a convenient initial data model for our work that can be scaled by Aqqaluk Trust and others to meet future and broader rematriation needs.

Check out the prototype at [https://rematriation.vtdataservices.org/](https://rematriation.vtdataservices.org/)

Special thanks to James Tuttle, the Associate Director of Library Data Strategies at Virginia Tech's University Libraries. James helped us create and host this archival content management system, as we plan, coordinate, and develop regional relationships to create a sustainable IT infrastructure.
